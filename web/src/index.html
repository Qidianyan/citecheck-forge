§ 1How it works

Three deterministic steps, every time a page or issue changes.

  1. Extract

    Parses citations in English, Vietnamese, Indonesian, Thai and Chinese — including EU articles, US FRCP rules, corporate policy keys ("Policy POL-7 §3") and ranges ("Articles 5–7").

  2. Link to authoritative text

    Resolves the exact statute and version from {{LAWS}} bundled official texts plus registered Confluence organisation policies.

  3. Verdict, H3 redirect & evidence

    Checks existence, in-force/archived status, quote fidelity and IDF clause misattribution ("did you mean Article 33?"), attaching official excerpts for human sign-off.
