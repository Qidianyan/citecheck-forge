POL-7 — Mekong Retail Pte. Ltd. Data Retention Policy
Internal policy page (fictional seed data for the CiteCheck dev site).
Register this page as an organisation source on the CiteCheck admin page.

§ 1 Scope
This policy covers all personal data processed by Mekong Retail Pte. Ltd. in Vietnam, Indonesia and Thailand.

§ 2 Lawful basis
Retail staff may process personal data only under a recorded lawful basis.

§ 3 Access rule
Retail staff may access customer personal data only for order fulfilment.

§ 4 Retention
Order records are deleted 24 months after the last purchase.

§ 5 Breach duty
Staff must report a suspected data breach to the privacy team within 24 hours.
